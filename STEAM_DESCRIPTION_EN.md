[h1]🛡️ Minidoracat Safe Spawn for B42[/h1]
[h3]By Minidoracat[/h3]

[hr][/hr]

[h2]✨ What is this[/h2]
Grants players a short invisibility protection on login or respawn: while the countdown runs,
zombies cannot see or hear you and will not attack, so you can safely take over your character
and leave a dangerous spawn point. Works for all players — no admin privileges required.

[h2]🧰 Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Login / respawn protection[/b]: activates automatically when joining a server or respawning, ends when the countdown expires
[*] [b]Real-time countdown[/b]: measured in REAL seconds (independent of the server's game-clock speed), starts only after loading finishes
[*] [b]New character bonus[/b]: fresh characters get longer protection (default 60s); returning characters get shorter (default 30s)
[*] [b]Countdown hints[/b]: overhead text every 10 seconds, per-second for the last 5 (no sound that attracts zombies)
[*] [b]Visual feedback[/b]: protected players appear semi-transparent
[*] [b]Admin tools[/b]: right-click menu to toggle invisibility instantly and view protection status
[*] [b]Fully configurable[/b]: everything adjustable through Sandbox Options
[*] [b]Singleplayer & multiplayer[/b]: full support for dedicated servers and multiplayer
[/list]

[h2]⚙️ Sandbox Options[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Enable Ghost on Spawn[/b]: enable/disable spawn protection (default: ON)
[*] [b]Veteran Ghost Time[/b]: protection for returning characters (REAL seconds, default 30, range 1-600)
[*] [b]New Character Ghost Time[/b]: protection for new characters (REAL seconds, default 60, range 1-600)
[/list]

[b]⚠️ Upgrading a server from pre-v1.5.0[/b]: the time unit changed from in-game minutes to real
seconds and the sandbox options were renamed — old values are ignored and fall back to the new
defaults (30/60s). Re-configure once if you had customized them.

[h2]📝 Known limits[/h2]
[list]
[*] In multiplayer, zombies simulated by another player's client right next to your spawn may still notice you (engine limitation)
[*] Protected players briefly keep vanilla ghost movement traits (no terrain/tree slowdown)
[/list]
