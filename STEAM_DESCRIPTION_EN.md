[h1]🛡️ Minidoracat Safe Spawn for B42[/h1]
[h3]By Minidoracat[/h3]

[hr][/hr]

Players get a short invisibility protection automatically when they log in or respawn, so you can safely take over your character and leave a dangerous spawn point.

[h2]📦 Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*] No other mods required; works in singleplayer and multiplayer, and in multiplayer the server must enable this mod
[*] Supported version: Build 42.16.1+
[*] [b]Add/remove mid-save:[/b] safe either way
[*] [b]Languages:[/b] 繁體中文, 简体中文, English, 日本語
[/list]

[h2]✨ Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Automatic protection[/b]: turns on at login or respawn and ends when the countdown runs out
[*] [b]Zombies don't notice you[/b]: they can't see or hear you and won't attack
[*] [b]Real-time countdown[/b]: unaffected by the server's clock speed, starts after loading finishes
[*] [b]Longer for new characters[/b]: both durations are adjustable in Sandbox Options
[*] [b]Clear status cues[/b]: semi-transparent character and a silent overhead countdown
[*] [b]Admin tools[/b]: right-click menu to toggle invisibility or check protection status
[/list]
⚠️ Protection is limited by the game engine and isn't absolute; the common reasons you might still get bitten are covered in the guide.
